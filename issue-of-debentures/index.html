Issue of debentures: journal entries for every case

A share makes you an owner. A debenture makes you a lender. The company must pay interest on debentures whether it earns a profit or not.

What a debenture is

A debenture is a written acknowledgement of a debt taken by a company, usually under its seal, with a fixed rate of interest and terms of repayment. Debenture holders are creditors of the company. Interest on debentures is a charge against profit: it is paid even in a loss year and is deducted before profit is found.

Shares and debentures compared

BasisSharesDebentures
HolderOwner (member)Creditor (lender)
ReturnDividend, out of profit, not fixed for equityInterest at a fixed rate, payable even without profit
Voting rightsEquity shareholders voteNo voting rights
RepaymentEquity is repaid only on winding up (or buy-back)Repaid on the due date
SecurityNot securedUsually secured by a charge on assets
On winding upPaid lastPaid before shareholders
Issue at discountNot allowedAllowed

Types of debentures

  • By security: secured (mortgage) and unsecured (naked).
  • By redemption: redeemable and irredeemable (perpetual).
  • By conversion: convertible into shares, or non-convertible.
  • By record: registered and bearer.
  • By coupon: specific-rate and zero-coupon.

When debentures are redeemable at a premium

If the company promises to repay more than the face value, the extra is a known loss from the day of issue. It is recorded at once by debiting Loss on Issue of Debentures A/c and crediting Premium on Redemption of Debentures A/c, which is a liability.

In case 5 the loss of ₹15,000 is the discount of ₹5,000 plus the redemption premium of ₹10,000. A sixth case, issue at a premium and redeemable at a premium, credits Securities Premium as well.

Writing off the discount or loss

Discount or loss on issue of debentures is a capital loss. It is written off in the year of issue, first out of Securities Premium, if any, and the remainder to the Statement of Profit and Loss as a finance cost.

Mar 31Securities Premium A/c   Dr.5,000
To Discount on Issue of Debentures A/c5,000
(Discount written off out of securities premium)

Issue for consideration other than cash

Example. Sahyadri Ltd. takes over the business of Konkan Traders: assets ₹4,50,000 and liabilities ₹50,000, for a purchase consideration of ₹4,40,000, paid by issuing 9% debentures of ₹100 each at par.

Net assets = ₹4,50,000 − ₹50,000 = ₹4,00,000. The price is ₹40,000 more, which is goodwill. (If the price were less than net assets, the difference would be credited to Capital Reserve.) Number of debentures = 4,400.

Issue as collateral security

A company taking a bank loan may give the bank its own debentures as additional (collateral) security. The bank can sell them only if the loan is not repaid. No interest is paid on such debentures. Either no entry is passed and the fact is stated in a note to the loan, or this entry is passed and reversed when the loan is repaid:

Journal
DateParticularsDr. (₹)Cr. (₹)
Debenture Suspense A/c   Dr.1,00,000
To 9% Debentures A/c1,00,000
(Debentures issued as collateral security for a bank loan)

Interest on debentures

Interest is calculated on the face value at the stated rate, whatever the issue price. On ₹1,00,000 of 9% debentures the yearly interest is ₹9,000, usually paid half-yearly as ₹4,500. Companies deduct income tax at source from the interest at the rate in force and pay it to the government; the entries show the gross interest as due, then the net payment and the tax deducted.

In the balance sheet

  • Debentures: under Non-current liabilities as Long-term borrowings (the part due within 12 months is shown under Current liabilities).
  • Premium on redemption: with the debentures, as a long-term liability.
  • Securities premium: under Reserves and Surplus.

Redemption of debentures. The rules on Debenture Redemption Reserve and on setting aside investments before redemption depend on the type of company and have been amended several times. Follow your board's or university's current syllabus for that part.

Questions students ask

Can debentures be issued at a discount?

Yes. Unlike shares, debentures may be issued at a discount. The discount is a capital loss, written off in the year of issue from securities premium and then from profit.

On what amount is debenture interest calculated?

On the face (nominal) value of the debentures at the stated rate, not on the issue price.

What is Loss on Issue of Debentures?

The total of discount allowed on issue and premium payable on redemption. It is recorded at the time of issue.
