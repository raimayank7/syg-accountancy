How to use a formula sheet

A formula is the last line of an idea, not a substitute for it. Learn each one from its worked example first, so that you know what every term means and where its figure comes from; then use this page for quick revision in the days before an examination. A good test: cover the right-hand column, and write each formula from memory. Mark the ones you miss and repeat only those the next day.

Three cautions. First, names differ between books: "revenue from operations" and "net sales" are the same thing in ratio questions, as are "trade receivables" and "debtors plus bills receivable". Second, when a question does not give opening figures, use the closing figure in place of an average and say so in a note. Third, always state the unit of your answer: a ratio as 2 : 1, a turnover as "times", a profitability ratio as a percentage.
